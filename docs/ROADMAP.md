# Gameplay variety

## Implemented

- Rotating score, color collection, jelly, blocker, and ingredient objectives.
- Layered ice, frosting, and crates with adjacent-match and special damage.
- Notched, narrow-bridge, and separated-island layouts with segment-aware gravity.
- Cherries and marked delivery exits.
- Chocolate that expands after a valid turn unless chocolate was cleared.
- Three temporary campaign perks, selected after each victory, with overlapping durations.
- Safe/challenge route choices with visible budgets, objectives, and perk-duration rewards.
- Authored conveyor, portal, delivery, and rotating-weak-spot boss puzzles.
- Campaign milestones from level 15 and a standalone fixed-seed puzzle room.
- Conveyor cycles after valid player turns and portal-connected gravity paths.
- UTC daily challenges with fixed resources, local best results, and versioned share links.
- A production combo laboratory with 14 prepared lessons and unlimited moves.
- Lifetime mastery recipes and four selectable board themes.
- Optional bonus exit through Bonus / Extra → Finish bonus.

The original mechanics enter gradually from level 2, then rotate through ten-level
families. Authored milestones supplement those families. Mixed levels require every
displayed objective before bonus play starts. Side modes isolate campaign resources;
mastery is earned in campaign play. Controls and descriptions support English and
Spanish. See the README for exact rules and docs/SAVES.md for compatibility.
